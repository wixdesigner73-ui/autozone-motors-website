FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?

Mechanical, electrical, AC, bodywork, alignment, detailing, tyres, rims, accessories, and car care.

Do you offer computerized diagnostics?

Yes, computerized systems for electrical, sensor, ABS, hybrid, and engine issues.

Can you service hybrid vehicles?

Yes, including battery checks, replacement, and electrical inspection.

What engine work do you do?

Oil changes, transmission fluids, throttle body tuning, injector and carbon cleaning, suspension, overhaul, and replacement.

What AC services are available?

Service, gas charging, recycling, compressor checks, and parts replacement.

Do you do alignment, tyre and brake work?

3D alignment, automated tyre changing, balancing, brake service, and pad replacement.

What bodywork do you handle?

Denting, painting, dent pulling, spot removal, sanding, MIG welding, and full paint.

Which coatings do you offer?

Ceramic, graphene, and paint protection film.

What do you sell?

Branded and Chinese tyres, alloy rims, upholstery, mats, sound systems, and LED and fog lights.

How do I book?

By phone, WhatsApp, or in person at the workshop.
